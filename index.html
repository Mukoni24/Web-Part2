Angela's Hope For Tomorrow Foundation

Bringing Hope, Changing Lives

The Angela's Hope Foundation is a non-profit organisation whose mission is to positively impact the lives of children, youths, and their families through sustainable community development activities. The Angela's Hope Foundation was established with the intention of solving issues such as poverty, illiteracy, unemployment among the youths, and lack of food.
