Frequently Asked Questions

How can I volunteer with Angela’s Hope Foundation?

You can volunteer by completing our enquiry form or contacting us directly. We welcome individuals who are interested in supporting our community programmes and activities.

How can I make a donation?

Donations can be made through our Donations page. You can contribute financially or donate useful items such as food, clothing and school supplies.

Who does the foundation support?

Angela’s Hope Foundation supports children, young people and families who may need educational, food, mentorship and community assistance.

What programmes does the foundation offer?

Our programmes include education support, food assistance, youth mentorship, skills development and community outreach.

Can my organisation partner with the foundation?

Yes. We welcome organisations, businesses and community groups that would like to support our programmes through partnerships, donations or other forms of collaboration.

How can I find out about upcoming events?

Upcoming activities and events are posted on our News & Events page. You can also contact us directly for more information.

How can I contact Angela’s Hope Foundation?

You can contact us through the contact details provided on our Contact Us page or submit an enquiry using the enquiry form.
