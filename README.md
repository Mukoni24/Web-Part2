## Project Overview 
### The organisation name: Angela ‘s Hope Foundation. 

The Angela’s Hope Foundation is a non-governmental organisation that seeks to enhance the welfare of children, youths, and their families through community development programs. The organisation’s operations revolve around socio-economic issues like poverty, lack of jobs, low levels of education and food scarcity. As an attempt to solve these problems, the organisation encourages education, skills development, community development and other projects that will positively impact community welfare. These areas reflect social responsibility principles which involve contributing to community welfare through education, job creation, skill development, donation and community-based projects among others.

### Brief History
Angela’s Hope Foundation is formed with the intent of overcoming socio-economic difficulties of the less fortunate such as poverty, unemployment, lack of education, and hunger. This foundation strives to help with community development through education, food, mentoring of the youth, skill development, and outreach in the community.

This foundation takes its lead from other foundations such as Afrika Tikkun (Anon., n.d.) which is a South African organisation formed in 1994 to alleviate the problem of poverty and inequality through education, healthcare, youth development, and skill development. Afrika Tikkun is a clear example of the positive change that can be achieved through partnership and investments in the communities. Angela’s Hope Foundation seeks to empower the children, youth, and families through giving them opportunities for education, independence, and hope for the future.
With the involvement of volunteers, donors, businesses, organisations in the community, and educational institutions, the programs of this foundation would definitely be enhanced.

### Mission Statement
The mission of Angela’s Hope Foundation is to make a difference in the lives of children, youth, and families through educational programs, food assistance, mentoring programs, skill development activities, and community development initiatives.

### Vision Statement
 Vision Statement of Angela’s Hope Foundation is building communities which are strong and sustainable, giving the children, youth, and their families an environment where they can make a difference.
 
### Target Audience 
The beneficiaries of the Angela’s Hope Foundation are numerous and range from children and youths to families, volunteers, donors, sponsors, community partners, and educational institutions. While children and youths stand to gain from education, mentoring, and skill development, families would be able to enjoy community-based assistance. In addition, volunteers would be able to make use of their expertise and help out with time, while sponsors and donors could provide funding and other forms of assistance.

## Website Goals and Objectives
### Website Goal 
#### Purpose of the Website:
The major purpose of the website is to develop a professional and user-friendly platform that connects Angela's Hope Foundation with its beneficiaries, volunteers, donors, and other community partners. This will enable easy access to information, communication, volunteering, and fundraising.

### 0bjectives 
#### The website aims to:
1. Increase Awareness by offering information on the foundation and its programmes.
2. Improve Communication through contact information, inquiries, social media, and newsletters.
3. Increase Volunteer involvement via an online volunteer registration system.
4. Support Fundraising through online donation system.
5. Improve Stakeholder involvement by keeping them informed through news, events, and programmes.
6. Enhance the foundation’ s online presence and accessibility of services offered by the foundation.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Performance of the website will be evaluated through:
1.	Website traffic – visitor count and number of pages viewed.
2. Volunteer sign-ups – number of sign-ups.
3.	Donations – number and value of donations.
4. Inquiries – number of inquiries.
5.	Newsletter’s sign-ups – number of newsletter subscribers.
6. User engagement – engagements on programmes, events, and social media sites.

## Proposed Website Features and functionality: 	
Angela’s Hope Foundation website is meant to be an easily accessible platform that can enable people to find out more information on the organization and its various activities. Some of the important features and functions of the website include:

1.	Home page - This page would provide introduction about Angela’s Hope Foundation by means of its logo, slogan, information and a community-based image. Navigation links will also be there for the convenience of the visitors.
   
2.	About Us – This page will contain information about the foundation, its history, its mission, its vision, and its purpose.
   
3.	Community Programmes - There will be an opportunity for visitors to learn about programs like education, food assistance, youth mentoring, skill development, and outreach programs.
   
4.	Gallery - In this section, photographs of the various activities and community programmes that the foundation undertakes will be displayed for visitors to see.
   
5.	News & Events – The latest updates regarding the foundation’s activities and events will be mentioned in this section.
    
6.	Volunteer Registration – Those who are willing to volunteer for the foundation shall have the option to learn more about volunteering opportunities and register themselves.
    
7.	Donations – The donations part of the website will contain information on how people can contribute to the foundation financially.
    
8.	Contact Us - Visitors can have access to contact information about the foundation and use the available means of communication to reach out.
    
9.	 Enquiries – Through an enquiry form, visitors will be able to contact the foundation with any queries or requests.
    
10.	Responsive Navigation – The navigation bar will contain links for the primary pages, and the responsiveness will enable the site to work well on all screen sizes.
    
11.	Call to Action Elements – Actions like volunteering and donations will make sure that people become part of the activities of the foundation and its programs.
